## Current Project

### UAV Powertrain Health Monitoring Framework

**Team:** Prissha Chawla (CS), Siddharth Urankar (CS), Ally Blair (ME)  
**Advisors:** Dr. Manish Kumar (Mechanical Engineering, UC), Dr. Manuel (ZHAW, Switzerland), Dr. Kulkarni (NASA Ames)

**Project Abstract**

As Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) become increasingly integral to logistics, surveillance, and emergency response, ensuring the reliability of their powertrain components, particularly motors and Li-ion batteries, is critical to safe and sustained operation. This work presents a comprehensive in-flight powertrain health monitoring framework that combines semi-supervised and supervised learning techniques to detect anomalies and predict degradation. Motor health is assessed using a semi-supervised one-dimensional Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) Autoencoder trained on healthy acoustic signatures of Brushless DC (BLDC) motors, enabling early fault detection without the need for large, labeled datasets. For battery health monitoring, a supervised Deep Operator Network (DeepONet) is trained on historical voltage and current data across multiple charge and discharge cycles to estimate battery degradation, predict Remaining Useful Life (RUL), and compute remaining flight time. The integration of both models enables real-time data-driven diagnostics of the UAV powertrain. This framework is designed for deployment within the In-time Aviation Safety Management System (IASMS), supporting both preflight readiness assessment and inflight anomaly detection, and improving UAV reliability in mission critical environments.

**Description**

- Ally will design and build the drones with multiple sensors and microphones under the guidance of Dr. Kumar and his PhD student, Luke. The drone(s) will be used to collect data over multiple runs.
- Propeller faults, bearing faults, and other faults will be induced and recorded in addition to healthy flight data.
- Part (a) will focus on acoustic data - sound of motors - which will be used to detect anomalies using a semi-supervised deep learning approach.
- Part (b) will focus on battery discharge data that will be used to predict RUL and flight time using supervised deep learning.
- The combination of motor and battery health monitoring will be tested on the drone both preflight and inflight. Data from various sensors will be recorded to further study anomaly detection and health monitoring.
